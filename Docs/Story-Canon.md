# Story Canon — The Second Dawn (29 Oct 2026)

> **Status: CANON for story.** This page replaces the earlier premise.
> It owns the premise, the cast, the artifact rules and the route. It does not own scene playback
> (see `Story-Scenes.md`) or node scripts (see `Story-Nodes.md`).
> **Built today: none.** The first build target is one node and one in-game scene.

---

## 1. The premise, in one paragraph

The Black Knight and his army have taken the kingdom and are one blow from ending it. In that moment the
king breaks **the Second Dawn**, an artifact that stores time. Time runs backward to before the campaign
began. The artifact shatters. One shard falls into **Greenwake Forest**, just outside the walls. The king
is spent and cannot raise an army without being seen. He sends only his King's Guard, along the road the
Black Knight will take, to stop him before he becomes strong.

---

## 2. What the player knows at the start

The player is told, and only told, these facts:

- The kingdom fell. Time went back. The king remembers what the others do not.
- The Black Knight will do it again.
- The king broke the artifact. Its pieces are lost.
- The Guard must move quietly and stop him early.

The player is **not** told:

- what a shard does,
- how time works,
- why the king is still alive,
- why a shard fell where it did,
- how many shards exist.

Do not explain these in dialogue. Show them when the game needs them.

## 4. The Second Dawn — artifact rules

These rules are fixed. If a later scene breaks one, the scene is wrong.

1. **It stores time.** Breaking it sends the world back once, to a set point.
2. **It broke into shards.** Each shard holds a piece of that stored time.
3. **A shard wakes matter.** It does not make life. It makes mud, roots and water move. This is why the
   slimes in Greenwake exist.
4. **Only the king remembers the first timeline.** The Black Knight does not. His campaign repeats.
5. **A shard can pull the Guard back to the last ground they held.** This is the in-world reason a wipe
   returns the party to the node. It is not explained until the player has lost once.
6. **Nobody lectures.** No character explains a shard, time, or the plot. The player learns from what
   happens and from one short line at the moment it matters.

## 6. Video and in-game split

| Where | What | Length | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Prerendered video** | Siege, artifact, time reversal, the king's order | 60-65 s | New Game only. Skippable. Never on Continue |
| **In-game scene** | The Guard arrives at Greenwake. First threat. First goal | 12-14 s | Stage 1 start, before wave 1 |

Rule: the video never explains a mechanic. It shows a shard fall into the forest. The in-game outro
reveals what a shard is.

### Video beat sheet

| Time | Shot |
|---|---|
| 0-6 | Kingdom morning. Market, banners, children. Calm |
| 6-14 | Black banners on the ridge. Smoke. Riders |
| 14-22 | Castle doors break. The Guard falls. The Black Knight enters |
| 22-30 | The king alone at the altar. He lifts the artifact. White light |
| 30-40 | Time reverses. Embers fly back into fires. Smoke unwinds. Sky runs backward |
| 40-48 | Throne room whole again. The artifact cracks. Shards scatter. One flies out toward the forest |
| 48-56 | The king on one knee, to the Guard. Two lines only |
| 56-62 | The Guard leaves at dawn. Title card |

The king has exactly two lines:

1. "He will come again. This time, before he is strong."
2. "I cannot send an army. He would see it coming. I send only you."

No narrator. No other dialogue. If the video needs a third line, the shot is wrong.

**Production note:** the video is the longest lead item in the plan. Do not block node 1 on it. Script it
now, ship a three-card text placeholder, make the final video last.

Rules:

---

## 8. Time rules (canon)

- One rewind happened. It cannot happen again at this scale.
- The king remembers. Nobody else does.
- The Black Knight repeats his campaign without knowing it was already won.
- Shards carry fragments of the stored time. They are the only "memory" in the world.
- A wipe rewinds the Guard, not the world. That is why the enemy is the same and the party keeps its gear.
